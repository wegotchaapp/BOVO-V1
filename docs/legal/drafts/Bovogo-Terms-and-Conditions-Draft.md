## 4 Passengers pets and luggage

Book the correct number of people, pets and bags. Each human passenger needs an available passenger seat. Each pet reservation uses two passenger-seat spaces and costs twice one human passenger’s mileage fare. Book a pet only in a vehicle that accepts pets, and make appropriate safe transport arrangements with the driver. Do not place an animal in a position that obstructs the driver or safe use of the vehicle.

Other riders may be shown that a booking includes a pet so they can consider this before booking. A pet tag describes the reservation and is not a guarantee about allergies or animal behavior. The pet pricing and pet-friendly rules do not override rights concerning service animals under applicable law. Contact support for an accessibility accommodation.

Luggage details help riders and drivers plan capacity. Confirm that your luggage can be transported safely; a selected bag count does not permit overloading the vehicle or blocking exits.

## 5 Mileage fare and checkout fees

The mileage fare for one human passenger is ($0.54 × that passenger’s verified traveled miles) ÷ 3. The divisor is always three and does not change with the number of people in the vehicle. For example, 180 miles gives a $32.40 human passenger fare, and 200 miles gives a $36.00 fare, before booking fees. One pet is charged twice the corresponding human passenger fare.

The checkout presents three components: the estimated mileage fare for the booked party, a $2 platform fee per booking, and a separately disclosed transaction fee. The proposed transaction fee is (2.9% × (mileage fare + platform fee)) + $0.30, rounded to cents. Any such fee is subject to applicable law, card-network rules and the payment provider’s requirements; only a lawful fee displayed before authorization may be charged.

For a 200-mile booking with one person and no pets, the mileage fare is $36.00, the platform fee is $2.00, and the proposed transaction fee is $1.40, for an estimated total of $39.40. This example is not a quote for any particular trip. There is no optional trip-insurance charge under this pricing model. Removing that add-on does not remove anyone’s legal obligation to maintain required vehicle or service insurance.

## 6 Authorization and final payment

Before payment, checkout must show the estimate and a higher maximum amount for you to approve. A payment authorization can temporarily reserve funds on your payment method. It is not necessarily the final charge. Do not approve the payment unless you agree to the displayed maximum.

For a completed ride, the mileage component is calculated using the driver’s recorded GPS route between that rider’s boarding and individual drop-off events. Straight-line distance or an undisclosed estimate must not silently replace missing route data. If tracking is incomplete or the amount is disputed, the booking requires review. The charge must stay within the amount you authorized; an additional charge requires separate valid authorization.

All of the mileage fare is allocated to the driver. The platform fee is allocated to Bovogo and the transaction-fee component retains its disclosed allocation. Driver payout timing can depend on completed payment processing, refunds, disputes and required account verification. Contact support about an incorrect charge or missing payment.

## 7 Cancellations and refunds

The cancellation time is compared with the trip’s scheduled departure time. The six-hour cutoff includes exactly six hours before departure. The departure time and its time zone must be made clear in the booking. Contact support if a schedule change or a technical failure affects your cancellation.

- Rider cancellation at least six hours before departure: the rider receives a 100% refund of the charged booking total, including the platform fee and transaction fee. If funds were only authorized, the authorization is released instead of issuing a refund of an uncaptured charge.

- Rider cancellation less than six hours before departure: the booking is nonrefundable under the standard cancellation rule. The disclosed cancellation mileage fare is allocated to the driver, while the existing platform and transaction-fee allocations remain unchanged. A late cancellation charge uses the booking’s approved cancellation amount, not invented traveled miles, and may not exceed the authorized maximum.

- Driver cancellation: affected riders receive a 100% refund of the charged booking total, including the platform fee and transaction fee, or release of an uncaptured authorization.

- Driver cancellation after a rider has already canceled late: the earlier rider also receives a full refund, including fees. The later driver cancellation overrides the earlier nonrefundable treatment.

Refunds are returned through the original payment method where possible. A release of a hold and a refund of a settled charge can appear differently on a bank statement. Bank and payment-provider processing times vary; approval of a refund does not mean it will appear immediately. These rules do not limit a refund or remedy required by law.
